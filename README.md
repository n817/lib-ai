# LibAI

**LibAI is an AI-powered personal library that lets you ask questions about your own documents.**

Upload books, notes, manuals, and other PDF files. LibAI processes the documents, breaks them into searchable chunks, and builds a knowledge base that you can interact with through a chat interface.

LibAI retrieves relevant information from your library to answer questions. If the answer cannot be found in your documents, LibAI clearly tells you that the information is not available instead of making it up.

## How It Works

1. Upload your PDF documents to your Library.
2. LibAI processes and indexes the documents.
3. Ask questions through the chat.
4. LibAI searches your documents for relevant information.
5. Get an answer based on your own sources - or a clear indication that the answer was not found.

## Key Features

- Personal document library
- PDF upload and processing
- AI-powered document retrieval
- Chat with your documents
- Answers grounded in your own sources
- No answers invented beyond the available documents
